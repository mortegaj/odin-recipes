Lassagna

Description

A homemade lasagna with layers of pasta, a rich meat and tomato filling, and creamy béchamel sauce. The melted cheese topping adds a golden, crispy finish.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets.
  • 500 g ground beef, or a mixture of ground beef and pork.
  • 700 g crushed tomatoes.
  • 1 onion.
  • 1 carrot.
  • 2 garlic cloves.
  • 150 g grated mozzarella.
  • 40 g grated Parmesan.
  • Olive oil, salt, black pepper, and oregano.
For the béchamel sauce:
  • 750 ml milk.
  • 60 g butter.
  • 60 g plain flour.
  • Salt, black pepper, and nutmeg.

Steps

  1. Make the filling. Finely chop the onion, carrot, and garlic. Sauté in a little olive oil for 10 minutes. Add the ground meat and cook until well browned. Stir in the tomatoes, season with salt, pepper, and oregano, and simmer for 25-30 minutes.
  2. Make the béchamel. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, and cook for 2 minutes. Gradually add the milk, stirring constantly, until smooth and creamy. Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
  3. Prepare the pasta. Cook or soak the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions.
  4. Assemble the lasagna. Spread a little béchamel over the bottom of a baking dish. Alternate layers of pasta, meat sauce, and béchamel. Finish with pasta, béchamel, and the grated cheeses.
  5. Bake and serve. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for 30–35 minutes. Briefly place under the grill if needed to brown the topping. Let rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
